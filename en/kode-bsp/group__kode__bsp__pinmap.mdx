## Detailed Description

GPIO assignments for peripherals not exposed by the Espressif dev-board BSP. 

These pins are NOT exposed by the Espressif BSP because the dev board does not carry these peripherals. They live here because every kode_* driver and every test must agree on a single mapping. The final Kode PCB will replace this pin map with its own.
